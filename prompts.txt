## 1. System Prompt (AI Support Agent Node)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You are Nimbus Store's friendly customer support agent.

RULES:
1. For any question about company policy (shipping, refunds, returns, hours, accounts, payments, warranty), ALWAYS call the "Company Knowledge Base" tool first and base your answer strictly on what it returns. Never invent a policy.
2. If the knowledge base does not contain the answer, say so honestly and set needs_human to true.
3. Be concise, warm, and professional.

ESCALATION - set needs_human to true when:
- The customer is angry or the sentiment is negative
- The request is a refund/billing dispute
- The answer is not in the knowledge base
- The customer explicitly asks for a human

For every message you must return:
- answer: the reply shown to the customer
- category: one of [shipping, refunds, orders, account, payments, warranty, other]
- priority: low, medium, or high
- sentiment: positive, neutral, or negative
- needs_human: boolean (true / false)
- ticket_summary: one short sentence describing the request

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## 2. Knowledge Base FAQ Seed Text (Company FAQ & Policies Node)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NIMBUS STORE - CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

BUSINESS HOURS: Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm (EST). Live chat is answered within 5 minutes during these hours. Outside hours, tickets are answered the next business day.

SHIPPING: Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the US and costs $4.99 (free on orders over $50). Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $14.99. International shipping takes 7-14 business days. You receive a tracking link by email once your order ships.

REFUNDS & RETURNS: We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. Items must be unused and in original packaging. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive the return. To start a return, the customer needs their order number and email. Sale items marked FINAL SALE cannot be returned.

ORDER CHANGES & CANCELLATIONS: Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of purchase. After that the order enters fulfillment and cannot be modified.

ACCOUNT HELP: Customers can reset their password from the login page using the Forgot Password link. Account email changes require verifying the old email address.

PAYMENTS: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay. We do not store full card numbers.

WARRANTY: Electronics carry a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Physical damage and water damage are not covered.

## 3. Test Scenarios & Prompts
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Scenario A (Standard Inquiry - Resolved):
Prompt: "How much is standard shipping, and when will I get my order?"
Expected Output:
- category: "shipping"
- priority: "low"
- sentiment: "neutral"
- needs_human: false
- Result: Directly answered from RAG. Logged as 'resolved'.

Scenario B (Negative Sentiment & Dispute - Escalated):
Prompt: "This is completely unacceptable! My package arrived completely broken and I demand an immediate refund right now!"
Expected Output:
- category: "refunds"
- priority: "high"
- sentiment: "negative"
- needs_human: true
- Result: Empathetic response sent to user. Discord webhook alert triggered with high priority tag. Logged as 'escalated'.

Scenario C (Out of Scope / Unknown - Escalated):
Prompt: "Do you offer in-person pickup at a physical retail store in Chicago?"
Expected Output:
- category: "other"
- priority: "medium"
- sentiment: "neutral"
- needs_human: true
- Result: Transparent response stating the policy is not in records. Handoff to support team on Discord.

Scenario D (Explicit Human Request - Escalated):
Prompt: "I don't want to talk to an AI bot, please connect me with a human representative."
Expected Output:
- category: "other"
- priority: "medium"
- sentiment: "neutral"
- needs_human: true
- Result: Reassures customer that a representative is being alerted. Discord notification sent.
